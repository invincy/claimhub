Death Intimation / Requirements

• Death certificate

• Original policy bond

• Death intimation letter from nominee

• Nominee photo ID and residence proof

• Nominee PAN, if TDS is to be deducted

• Nominee bank passbook or cheque

• If nominee is minor: appointee PAN, KYC, bank details, guardianship proof where needed

• Accidental death: FIR / PIR / PMR, chemical analysis, newspaper cutting, DL, statement of relatives, panchnama, investigation report

• Suicide: FIR / PIR / PMR, chemical analysis, statement of relatives, panchnama, investigation report

• Hospital death: discharge summary + applicable claim forms

• Lost policy bond: nominee letter, notarized indemnity bond, claimant ID and residence proof
